About Earthling Official & Our Editorial Standards

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Earthling Official is an independent, non-commercial advocacy and archive project covering missing persons awareness, platform accountability, and public digital discourse. This page explains who publishes this site, why, and how information is verified.

Why This Site Doesn't Use a Personal Byline

The person behind Earthling Official has received direct threats after public reporting and commentary work — including being publicly mischaracterized by a subject covered on this site. For personal safety, content here is published under the site name rather than a personal name. This is a safety decision, not an attempt to avoid accountability: the site maintains a consistent identity, a documented publishing history, and a public contact channel below for verification, corrections, or press inquiries.

A note on safety: Pseudonymous publication for personal safety is a recognized and legitimate journalistic practice, used by reporters covering harassment campaigns, organized crime, extremism, and other subjects where public identification creates real risk. It does not change the standard this site holds itself to for accuracy and sourcing.

What This Site Covers

  • Missing persons awareness — currently centered on the active case of Sally Grace Contarino (LVMPD Case #LLV260600083886)
  • Platform accountability reporting — documenting how social platforms handle missing persons content, moderation, and advertiser disputes
  • Public discourse and digital culture commentary, including satire and interactive media

Missing persons content on this site is kept factually grounded and links directly to the relevant law enforcement agency for tips or information — this site is a visibility and awareness effort, not an investigative authority, and defers to police on all case facts.

How Information Is Verified

  • Case details (names, dates, case numbers) are sourced from official police releases or confirmed news reporting, and cross-checked before publishing
  • Claims about platform behavior (ad restrictions, account flags, support responses) are documented with screenshots and timestamps at the time of the incident
  • Uncertain claims are stated as uncertain — this site avoids presenting speculation as confirmed fact, and flags where causation can't be proven
  • Corrections are made when errors are identified — see contact details below

Non-Commercial & Non-Partisan

Earthling Official is not commercially sponsored and does not accept payment for coverage. Missing persons advocacy content is run for awareness purposes only. Commentary and satire content elsewhere on the site is clearly distinct from case reporting and is not intended to be read as factual claims about real individuals beyond what is explicitly sourced.

Contact & Corrections

For corrections, tips, verification requests, or press inquiries, reach Earthling Official via TikTok: @earthling_official.
